PROMPT: REDESIGN THE CUSTOMER'S SNOW SCREENS (HOME CARD + SNOW SERVICE SCREEN)

I run a lawn and snow company. I tell my customers: "Sit back, open the app, and watch your jobs get done." The customer portal has to feel like that promise: calm, confident, proof of work at a glance. Design two iPhone screens (portrait, light theme, clean white cards on a warm off-white background, premium native-iOS look, generous spacing, big readable type, subtle shadows). Snow uses a calm blue accent; the rest of the app is dark green.

WHAT IS WRONG WITH THE CURRENT SCREENS (so you know what to fix)
1. Home: a Snow property is shown as one more "Active Project" card with a "Snow" tag. Snow is not a project. It has no progress, so the card feels empty and meaningless ("Active", an address, a vague sentence).
2. The Snow screen is an empty box that says "No snow updates yet", and then two huge notification switches take over the screen. Notification settings do not belong here. They belong in Settings.
3. When there IS an update it is just a flat card: "Plowing done", an address, a date, and a few thumbnails. No sense of the storm, no timeline, no story, nothing that says "we handled it".
4. Nothing tells the customer what is happening RIGHT NOW (storm coming, crew on the way, crew on site, done) or how much service they have had this season.
5. Capitalisation is inconsistent ("David'S Home" in one place, "david's home" in another). Use clean title case everywhere.

SCREEN 1: HOME (the customer's dashboard)
- Greeting: "Welcome back, David" (small grey "Welcome back", big bold name).
- A single "Right now" status strip at the very top when Snow applies. It changes with real status: "All clear. Last service: Tue, Oct 21 at 5:40 AM", or "Crew on the way", or "Crew on site at 205 Cathy Ln since 4:12 AM", or "Done: plowed and salted. See photos". Blue when a storm is active, calm green/grey when all clear. Tapping it opens the Snow screen.
- Then SEPARATE sections, each with a small uppercase label, not one mixed "Active Projects" list:
    SNOW: one compact card per Snow property (property name, address, the services it gets as small chips: Plowing, Shoveling, Salting, and a one-line "Last service Tue 5:40 AM").
    MAINTENANCE: recent visits card, only if they have maintenance.
    PROJECTS: their landscaping projects with the progress bar, only if they have any.
  A section never shows when it would be empty.
- Bottom tabs as today: Home, Messages, Account.

SCREEN 2: SNOW SERVICE (opened from the Snow card or the status strip)
Top to bottom:
1. Back arrow and the property name as the title, address under it in grey. If the customer has more than one Snow property, a small switcher (pill tabs) under the title.
2. "Right now" card (the hero). One clear state with an icon, a one-line headline and a time, for example:
     - Quiet: "No storm right now. We're watching the forecast." with the last service time.
     - Dispatched: "A storm is being handled. Crew assigned."
     - On site: "Crew on site since 4:12 AM."
     - Finished: "Plowing finished at 5:40 AM" with a small row of the first photos.
3. "This season" summary: three small stat tiles in a row: number of services, last service (date), photos. Real numbers only.
4. "Included at your property": the services this property gets as clean chips (Plowing, Shoveling, Salting).
5. TIMELINE (the main content). Grouped by day (and storm name when there is one), newest first, for example "Tuesday, Oct 21 · Oct 21 Plowing". Each entry is a card on a vertical line with a dot:
     - service icon + verb ("Plowed", "Shoveled", "Salted"), the time, and a green "Completed" check
     - a horizontal photo strip (the photos the owner approved and sent), tappable to a full-screen gallery you can swipe through, with a small "4 photos" count
     - a short note if the crew left one
   Older entries load as you scroll; show the 5 latest first and a "Show earlier" button.
6. Empty state (no service yet this season): not a sad empty box. Show a friendly 3-step "How it works": 1) The crew is dispatched when snow falls, 2) You'll get a message when they arrive, 3) When it's done you'll see photos here. Plus the included services.

NOTIFICATIONS
Do NOT put notification switches on either screen. Add one quiet row at the very bottom of the Snow screen: "Notification settings" with a chevron, which opens Settings (Account) where the switches live. In Settings the Snow notifications are a normal grouped list: "Crew arrives" and "Service finished", each with a standard small iOS switch and one grey explanation line.

STYLE RULES
- No decoration for its own sake. Every element must come from real data. Never invent names, times, photos, counts or storm names.
- One primary idea per card. Plenty of white space. No heavy borders.
- Photos are the proof of work, so give them room: rounded corners, a tidy strip, a nice full-screen viewer.
- Large touch targets, high contrast, readable on a phone at arm's length.
- Title case for names ("David's Home", "205 Cathy Ln").

STATES TO MOCK UP (please show all of these)
A. Home, Snow customer with one Snow property and one landscaping project, status strip "All clear".
B. Home during a storm: status strip "Crew on site since 4:12 AM".
C. Snow screen, finished storm with photos and a full timeline of several days.
D. Snow screen, no service yet this season (the friendly empty state).
E. Snow screen, crew on site (hero card in its active state).
F. The full-screen photo viewer.
G. Settings: the Snow notifications group.
