PROMPT FOR AN AI VISUAL: the "Storm setup" screen (Snow Mode, owner)

Design a mobile app screen (iPhone portrait, 390x844) for a snow-plowing business owner. App name: Brighton Lawn + Landscape, Snow Mode. Style: clean, calm "icy blue" look. A soft winter photo of snow-covered pine trees fades into the page behind the header only. Below it the page is a very light blue-grey with white rounded cards (16px corners, thin borders, no heavy shadows). Dark navy text, one strong blue accent for buttons. Big, thumb-friendly buttons. Friendly, not corporate.

WHERE IT IS
Snow Mode > Storms > tap a storm that is still a DRAFT (for example "Oct 20 Plowing"). It is the page where the owner gets a storm ready before sending the crews out. Nothing is running yet.

WHAT IT DOES
The owner builds the route for one storm, top to bottom, then dispatches it. The screen is a single scrolling page:

1. HEADER over the winter photo: a round back button on the left, a small pill saying "Draft" on the right. Big bold storm name "Oct 20 Plowing" with the service "Plowing" under it.

2. ROUTE NAME: the route's name in small text with a pencil icon (tap to rename, for example "David's route").

3. CREW CARD (white card):
   - "Crew" with a line such as "No crew" and a blue "+ Add Crew" button on the right. Tapping it opens a short list of snow-team workers to add. Added workers appear as rows with a small "Remove" link.
   - Below that, "Crew lead: who runs the route" with the chosen person's name (or an orange "Assign a crew lead" if nobody is picked yet) and a down chevron to pick from a list. A crew lead is required before dispatch.

4. PROPERTIES section: the label "PROPERTIES" (small caps). Empty state: "No properties yet. Tap Add Property below." Once added, each property is a numbered row (1, 2, 3) with name, address, a tiny camera icon with "Photos required" or "No photos required", and little arrows to move it up or down and an X to remove. Rows can be dragged to reorder. A full-width blue "+ Add Property" button sits under the list; tapping it shows a checklist of properties that offer plowing, with Cancel and Add buttons.

5. A small grey note: "5 properties offer plowing." (or "No active properties offer plowing yet. Turn the service on for a property first.")

6. BOTTOM ACTIONS: a large full-width blue button "Dispatch storm" with a send icon, and under it a plain text button "Delete draft".

NOT on this screen: no progress dashboard, no history list, no stats. Those only appear after the storm is dispatched.

Show two versions: (A) empty draft just created, (B) filled in with a 2-person crew, a lead chosen, and 4 properties.
