SNOW WORKER SIDE: WHAT I CHECKED AND HOW TO TEST IT ON YOUR PHONE
(2026-10-21, branch fix/snow-audit / PR #155. Nothing here has been seen on a phone.)

WHAT A WORKER SEES (3 tabs)
  Route       only if the owner put them on a route as lead OR crew. Otherwise the tab is hidden and they land on Time Clock.
  Time Clock  Clock In / Clock Out and this week's shifts.
  Settings    profile card, email and role, snow services (read-only), Privacy/Terms/Help, Sign Out, Delete Account.

WHAT I CHECKED (database, automated, all passing)
  Multi-service storms (56), crew on routes (47), clock-in tagged to a storm (21), shift corrections (11), snow gaps (12),
  stop notes and photos (11), combined routes (20), worker auto-enroll (16), clock-in needs work (46),
  permissions and cleanup (18), crew push notifications (14).
  One test was stale (it called an internal function the new permissions correctly lock); I pointed it at the real one.

WHAT I CHANGED TODAY ON THE WORKER SIDE
  Settings had four ways into the same "edit profile" screen (the card, plus Photo, Name and Phone rows). Now there is the
  card only, with one line under it: "Tap to change your name, phone or photo. The office sees them."

RULES THE CODE ENFORCES (so you know what is "correct" while testing)
  - Only the route LEAD can press I'm Here, add photos, Finish Stop, or ask to skip. Crew see the route and a note
    saying "You're on this route's crew. The route lead marks arrivals, finishes stops and adds photos."
  - Stops are done in order, one at a time. Finish Stop is blocked until the minimum photos are added.
  - Clock In only works when the worker is on a route in a dispatched storm. An open shift always keeps its Clock Out.
  - A storm that has ended (or a cancelled route) shows a notice and no buttons.
  - Pull down on the Route tab to refresh. The Route tab also re-checks every minute.

PHONE TEST SCRIPT (needs a NEW build; use 2 phones or 2 accounts: owner + worker lead + worker crew)
  A. Setup (owner phone)
     1. Create a storm, add properties, make a route, pick a lead and one crew member, tap Dispatch.
  B. Push (both worker phones, app closed)
     2. Lead gets a push about the dispatch with the route name and stop count.
     3. Crew gets a push saying they are crew on that route.
     4. Tapping either push opens the storm screen.
  C. Lead flow
     5. Route tab shows "Next stop" with name, address, owner note, Directions.
     6. Directions opens maps.
     7. Tap I'm Here -> status changes to "You're here".
     8. Try Finish Stop with no photos -> blocked, message says how many more photos.
     9. Add photos, add a note, Finish Stop -> next stop appears, count goes up, owner dashboard updates.
    10. Ask to skip a stop -> owner sees the request.
    11. Finish the last stop -> "Every stop on this route is done."
  D. Crew flow
    12. Route tab shows the route but NO I'm Here / Finish / photo buttons, only the crew note.
    13. Crew can still use Directions and see instructions.
  E. Time clock (either worker)
    14. Clock In works while storm is dispatched; Clock Out ends it and hours add to "this week".
    15. Before dispatch (or after the storm ends) Clock In is not offered.
    16. Forget to clock out -> owner can correct it in More > Snow hours.
  F. Settings
    17. Tap the profile card -> change photo/name/phone -> Save -> screen closes, keyboard gone.
    18. Services list matches what the owner gave. Sign Out, Privacy, Terms, Help, Delete Account each appear once.
  G. Edge cases
    19. Owner removes the worker's snow access -> worker is signed out of Snow Mode or sees no routes.
    20. Owner cancels the route mid-storm -> worker sees "This route was cancelled."
    21. Airplane mode: I'm Here / Finish should show a clear error, not freeze.

WHAT I COULD NOT VERIFY
  Anything visual or on a real device: push delivery to a locked phone, camera and photo upload, Directions handoff,
  and how the screens look. The database rules above are tested; the screens are only type-checked.
